About Mindfulness
In the rush of modern life, stress and distraction pull us further from ourselves — and from the peace we already carry within. Mindfulness is the art of returning: to the present moment, to stillness, to our truest selves. Join us as experienced Venerable Masters guide you through mindful walking practice, transforming every step across the Brooklyn Bridge into a path toward clarity and well-being.
Mindfulness & Giving
Compassion and generosity are not qualities we must acquire — they are already woven into our nature. Yet the pressures of daily life can bury them beneath layers of stress and anxiety. When we practice mindfulness and meditation, releasing negativity and returning to stillness, our innate care for others naturally rises and flows outward. That is why giving is at the heart of this walk.
For each completed walk, the organizer will make a $1 donation to support individuals with autism and their families.

About WBA
The Western Buddhist Association (WBA) is a non-profit Buddhist organization founded in New York City in 1998. Rooted in the traditions of Chinese Buddhism, WBA operates Fanyin Temple — a place of spiritual practice, cultural celebration, and community service in the heart of New York. We share the teachings of the Buddha, foster interfaith dialogue, and serve the broader community through compassionate action.
