About WBA
The Western Buddhist Association (WBA) is a non-profit Buddhist organization founded in New York City in 1998. Rooted in the traditions of Chinese Buddhism, WBA operates Fanyin Temple — a place of spiritual practice, cultural celebration, and community service in the heart of New York. We share the teachings of the Buddha, foster interfaith dialogue, and serve the broader community through compassionate action.
