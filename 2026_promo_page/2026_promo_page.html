About Mindfulness
Mindfulness is the practice of bringing conscious, non-judgmental awareness to the present moment. By intentionally sensing each footstep and each breath, we learn to gently release the busyness of the mind and return to stillness. Walking across the Brooklyn Bridge, the city becomes a quiet backdrop — and every step becomes an opportunity to meet yourself right where you are. Mindfulness needs no special setting; it lives in each step you choose to take with presence.

About WBA
The Western Buddhist Association (WBA) is a non-profit Buddhist organization founded in New York City in 1998. Rooted in the traditions of Chinese Buddhism, WBA operates Fanyin Temple — a place of spiritual practice, cultural celebration, and community service in the heart of New York. We share the teachings of the Buddha, foster interfaith dialogue, and serve the broader community through compassionate action.
